# Roadmap

Ideas in rough priority. Pick what earns its place after a few weeks of real use.

1. **Live brief inside the app.** Today reads `data/brief.json` (sample). Deliver the real brief privately: either a private host that serves the routine's JSON, or a small authenticated endpoint.
2. **Sync across devices.** Today everything is local. Add optional sync (Firebase, Supabase, or a single encrypted file in Drive) once a second device matters. Keep export/import as the safety net.
3. **Deadline pushes.** A small server or scheduled routine that sends a notification a few hours before a deadline or when a streak is about to break.
4. **Weekly review draft.** A routine that reads the week's stats and journal and drafts the "what went well / what to improve" prompts for the user to edit.
5. **Spaced repetition from notes.** Turn library highlights and "today I learned" entries into flashcards with one tap.
6. **Goal check-ins.** Quarterly review flow that scores each goal and asks what to keep, change or drop.
7. **Health and sleep log.** Optional module with sleep, exercise and energy trends against habits and mood.
8. **Search.** One box that searches tasks, journal, notes, cards and the log.
9. **Accessibility pass** and a larger-text setting.
